GROUP LEAD

Dr Andrew Stewart

UCL Chemistry · TEM Facility Manager

Andrew’s research connects electron crystallography, coherent diffractive imaging and electron optics.

He leads AMCLab and manages the transmission electron microscopy facility in UCL Chemistry. His work combines microscopy experiments with simulation and open-source analysis, with a particular interest in extracting useful information from small crystals and beam-sensitive materials.
